# LaunchMind AI Startup Guide

To build a successful startup, founders should focus on finding a burning user problem and establishing product-market fit (PMF).

## Core Startup Steps
1. **Identify Customer Pain Point**: Talk to users, conduct interviews, and find out what they struggle with daily.
2. **Design a Value Proposition**: Formulate a clear statement explaining how your product solves their problem better than existing alternatives.
3. **Build a Minimum Viable Product (MVP)**: Create the simplest version of the product that delivers value and test it with real users quickly.
4. **Iterate Based on Feedback**: Pivot or iterate based on actual product usage and customer input.
5. **TAM SAM SOM**:
   - TAM: Total Addressable Market (Total global demand for the product).
   - SAM: Serviceable Addressable Market (The segment of the TAM targeted by your products/services within your geographical reach).
   - SOM: Serviceable Obtainable Market (The portion of SAM you can realistically capture).
